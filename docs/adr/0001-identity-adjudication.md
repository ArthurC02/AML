# ADR-0001: Evidence-driven identity adjudication

- Status: Accepted. The scoring rule is decided (2026-11-26) and built as a trial calculation in measurement only (`Core/Scoring/ScoringRule.cs`); its weights are not approved, and until approval the stage 3 table decides and no MATCH is emitted.
- Date: 2026-11-02; updated 2026-11-09 (merged former ADR-0002, 0003, 0018, 0019), 2026-11-19 (values stated in attached news), 2026-11-26 (a deterministic scoring rule decides the match; no model output is the decision or the score; the proposed weights are tried in measurement only; automatic MATCH allowed in the match band; decisiveness is a system goal and no blanket human review for Sanction, PEP or RCA under the rule), 2026-11-27 (a Profile DOB more than two years from the customer's is a validated hard conflict, NO_MATCH; two years or less is a birth-date agreement; no blanket human review for Sanction, PEP or RCA today either: an identity NO_MATCH gives NO_HIT for every list type; birth dates and dated ages stated in attached news or on a received page, all more than two years off and the Profile without a DOB of its own, conclude NO_MATCH; a page's within two years neutral; a customer under 14 on the header date of an attached item naming the Profile person, under a full name, as an accused or a company officer is an advisory hint, REVIEW, until real labelled Cases measure it)

## Context

Name-screening systems often reduce identity to one similarity score. Such a score cannot explain why a pair matched, hides conflicting facts and mixes retrieval ranking with identity. Many weak agreements (same name, gender, nationality) can look convincing together even when one reliable fact proves two records are different people. A score that is a sum of named rule items, each from one piece of evidence with its reason code, has none of these faults: every point can be traced, a conflict is a visible negative item, and a veto stays a veto.

The bank's legacy rules (`Rule.md`) describe an earlier product: an LLM reads each case, writes an Excel report with a match/no-match recommendation and two probabilities, and humans score the report against a 100-point rubric. Many of its evidence rules are sound and are in the feature specs; its decision mechanism is not adopted.

Fellegi-Sunter, which sums per-field weights log₂(m/u), pays off only once m and u are estimated and calibrated against labelled cases. No population distribution and no labelled set exist yet.

On 2026-11-26 the user decided that everything is scored by a scoring rule and that the score decides whether a case matches (D2), made by a deterministic algorithm, never by a model's judgment (D4). The weights and thresholds are not yet approved.

## Decision

- **Explicit records, decided by a scoring rule** ([scoring rule](../feature/scoring-rule.md)). Adjudication works on CandidateProfile, Evidence, Conflict and SystemDecision. The identity decision is the band of an identity score: the sum of rule items, one per evidence group, each with its points, its source and a reason code in the code catalog; validated hard conflicts and caps stay outside the sum. Every decision lists its items, vetoes, unknown fields, reason codes and scoring-policy version ([explainability](../feature/identity-adjudication.md#explainability)). Retrieval scores (BM25, displayed similarity) rank candidates only and are never part of the identity score.
- **Deterministic, never a model's verdict** (D4). The same inputs under the same rule version give the same score and the same decision. No model output is the decision, the score or one of its items as such. A model may appear only upstream, as an extractor of fields and facts from text (persons, attributes, events, legal stage); what it reads enters the rule as an input marked as model-read, with its provenance, and the rule fixes what it is worth. A missing model answer stays missing and scores nothing. The score is points, never a probability and never shown as a percentage.
- **Three ordered stages** remain: Hard Conflict Gate (vetoes), Evidence Strength Assessment (rule items), Decision Policy (bands). A confirmed hard conflict cannot be offset by weak supporting attributes. Each hard-conflict rule names its evidence and policy version and carries its own validation status; only validated rules close a pair automatically, unvalidated ones contribute negative points but hold the pair at REVIEW ([hard conflict gate](../feature/identity-adjudication.md#hard-conflict-gate)). A rule is validated by a Golden Case or by the user's decision.
- **Birth dates within two years agree, further apart conclude** (user, 2026-11-27: 「生日兩年內都可以接受」, 「兩年以內可以判定符合」). Every Profile DOB more than two calendar years from the customer's, compared at the coarser precision so that a possible difference of two years or less never counts, is the validated hard conflict `FULL_DOB_CONFLICT` / `DOB_CONFLICT`: NO_MATCH today, in the stage 3 path and as a veto of the score. A different DOB within two years is a birth-date agreement at the weakest level (`DOB_AGREES_WITHIN_TOLERANCE`, LOW, as a birth year). The agreement side applies to the Profile's own field. The conflict side also applies to what attached news or a received page states next to the name (user, 2026-11-27): every comparable stated birth date and age at the report date more than two years from the customer's DOB is the validated hard conflict `STATED_DOB_CONFLICT`, NO_MATCH, unless the Profile has a DOB of its own (it then agrees, and the text may be about someone else) or a document agrees. Within two years a stated value keeps its rules: attached news its difference or compatibility, a page's birth date neutral, never agreement (on the synthetic pages agreement would lift 12 same-name strangers). A value not comparable (no report date) changes nothing. Measured: on the synthetic pages 10 strangers concluded, on the synthetic list set 34, none the same person ([two-year conflict on stated values](../feature/identity-adjudication.md#two-year-conflict-on-stated-values)); not measured, and accepted: an age as of a past event without 時年, or a traditionally counted age at the boundary, can close the same person. Accepted cost: a same-name stranger born within two years agrees on the birth date, and a Profile DOB entered more than two years wrong excludes the right person ([birth-date tolerance](../feature/identity-adjudication.md#birth-date-tolerance)). The document conflict stays pending.
- **A child named as the accused or the officer of a dated item is a hint, not a conclusion** (user, 2026-11-27). The shape is the vendor's attached news with a dated header on a Profile without a birth date of its own. When the customer, born on a known day, was under 14 at most on the header date (to the day) of an attached item or a received page's publication date, and in that item a suspect or company officer word stands directly before a whole full-name mention of the Profile person (read at the mention only, with the guards of the spec), the pair carries the advisory conflict `AGE_IMPLAUSIBLE_AT_DOCUMENT` and stays REVIEW (stage 3 row 1); it is not a hard conflict and never NO_MATCH. Advisory because the rule reads natural language with closed word lists known to be incomplete, and its error rate is not measured on real labelled Cases with the bank's decisions. It becomes a concluding rule only after such a measurement ([implausible age at a dated document](../feature/identity-adjudication.md#implausible-age-at-a-dated-document-advisory)).
- **Points follow strength, strength follows Fellegi-Sunter order.** Points per group and level (`LOW | MEDIUM | HIGH | UNIQUE`) keep the order of the chance of an accidental agreement, so calibrated weights can later replace them as a new rule version. Hit prerequisites (NAME, NATIONALITY, GENDER) score no points (the bank's rule); they are preconditions of a band. Fellegi-Sunter with term-frequency adjustment and calibration waits for Gate D; until then `FellegiSunter` stays a calibration tool outside every result.
- **The score decides once its weights are approved.** The proposed weights are used for trial calculation: the score is computed and compared with the [stage 3 table](../feature/identity-adjudication.md#stage-3-table) in measurement only, and the table decides, until the user approves the weights and the proposed switch gates pass ([validation](../feature/scoring-rule.md#validation)).
- **A value stated in a source is weaker than a Profile field.** An age, birth date or gender written next to a person's name in a page is evidence ([ADR-0012](0012-sources-and-web-information.md)), but it is the person's only by standing next to the name. Agreement is at most `MEDIUM`, scaled by the source's reliability, and never a direct identifier; a difference is a soft conflict that never takes a pair below REVIEW, save the two-year conflict above ([spec](../feature/identity-adjudication.md#birth-date-and-gender-stated-in-a-source)).
- **Automatic MATCH in the match band** (user, 2026-11-26). When the identity score reaches the MATCH band and no veto or cap applies, the algorithm concludes MATCH without a reviewer; with a HIT condition the pair result is HIT. It takes effect only when the score becomes the decision, after a measured automatic MATCH precision; today no MATCH is emitted. It applies to every list type. A MATCH is an identification, not a disposition ([ADR-0004](0004-identification-only.md)).
- **No blanket human review for any list type** (user, 2026-11-27: asked whether to lift the human review of a Sanction, PEP or RCA candidate concluded NO_MATCH, answered 「lift it」). The algorithm's conclusion stands whatever the list type: an identity NO_MATCH gives the [combination matrix](../feature/data-contracts.md#combination-matrix)'s NO_HIT on a Sanction, PEP or RCA condition, today under the stage 3 table, and an automatic MATCH of the score will not be held either. Accepted cost: a wrong automatic NO_MATCH on a sanctioned person is the most expensive error the system can make; what stands against it is that only validated hard conflicts conclude NO_MATCH (impossible age; DOBs more than two years apart, with the gate's downgrades). Not yet shown: no real Sanction or PEP Golden Case exists, and a list's placeholder 1 January birth date is read as a real date. A raised Sanction condition still makes a REVIEW pair HIGH priority.
- **Decisiveness is a goal** (user, 2026-11-26: a system that leaves everything to review is conservative but worthless). The rule exists to conclude MATCH or NO_MATCH wherever the evidence allows; REVIEW is the remainder, not the default. The decided rate is the primary identity measure, guarded by Auto-MATCH and Auto-NO_MATCH precision ([metrics](../plan/poc-delivery-plan.md#metrics)); a missing value still never counts against a pair.
- **PROBABLE_MATCH is internal.** It ranks REVIEW first; consumers receive REVIEW ([combination matrix](../feature/data-contracts.md#combination-matrix)).

## Consequences

- More data is stored per decision (one row per rule item); that is the price of traceability.
- Every weight, threshold and veto is testable with Golden Cases and readable by the business; a changed weight is a new scoring-policy version recorded in lineage.
- Every new rule-item, band and veto code goes into the code catalog with the build; a code without a catalog entry is a bug.
- An automatic MATCH is only as safe as its band: Auto-MATCH precision on labelled pairs becomes a release measure, beside the decided rate it guards.
- The PROBABLE_MATCH band, the NO_MATCH band and the 65% ruling stay [open questions](../plan/open-questions.md#identity-policy) until GC-06/GC-07 and labelled pairs exist.
