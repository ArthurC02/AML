# ADR-0012: Sources, web information and the news digest

- Status: Accepted. Web search and web identity evidence are decided but not built yet; until they are, the [news digest](../feature/watchlist-and-adverse-information.md#news-digest) and the [news models](../feature/watchlist-and-adverse-information.md#news-models) read only the news attached to a Profile. The news models feed no decision; the two stage models, when configured, vote with the rules on the legal stage ([three readers](../feature/watchlist-and-adverse-information.md#three-readers)); of the rule readings, the age, birth date and gender written next to the Profile person's name in attached news are identity evidence.
- Date: 2026-11-02; updated 2026-11-10 (the small models read attached news beside the rules), 2026-11-19 (birth date and gender in attached news are identity evidence), 2026-11-20 (a person-stage classifier and a large language model vote with the rules on the legal stage)

## Context

PoC input is supplied files. Later the bank may integrate by API, and data may need to be crawled.

Reviewers read the news and judgments about a listed person to learn who is involved, in what role and what happened: searched, detained, indicted, convicted. The same pages often settle identity: an age, a title, a place or a court case that fits or does not fit the customer. A Profile carries at most one attached article today, and several reports of one event are common. Results must be reproducible, explainable and run inside the bank. A large language model is not excluded; whether one is used is decided by measurements on the annotated documents. Measured per person on eight annotated sets (1,733 persons), the rules give the stage of 1,567, a person-stage classifier (bert-base-chinese fine-tuned on document and person) 1,439, and Gemma 4 12B (one call per document) 1,589; each is right where the others fail often enough that a vote of the three (at least two alike) gives 1,617, and where all three agree they are right 99.5 % of the time or more. On the held-back report set the vote gives 335 of 362 where the rules give 295; on the held-back court releases 137 of 148 where the rules give 140. Models come under clean licences ([ADR-0015](0015-licences.md)). Customer data stays inside the bank ([security and privacy](../feature/security-and-privacy.md)).

## Decision

- **Ports.** Customer and source data come through `ICustomerSource` and `IProfileSource` in Core. Another acquisition path is a new Infra implementation; normalization and adjudication do not change.
- **Web search starts from list-side data.** The system follows what the Profile already holds to the web: its names and aliases, titles, places and source URLs, searched on public judgment and news sites through the polite crawler ([web text](../feature/web-text.md)). Nothing from the customer side (name, date of birth, identifiers) is sent out.
- **Reading.** Pages are read by rules and the small models into people, roles and events with their dates, agencies, offences, amounts and penalties, and into identity facts about the listed person (age or birth year, title, place, nationality). Every fact keeps its sentence and source.
- **Rules and models side by side.** The rule digest and the models' readout are kept apart in the digest, each with its own provenance; neither overrides the other. The models' known errors are corrected by fixed rules only where a correction measurably helped on the annotated documents, and a rule result is not merged into the models' (on judgments and press releases it made them worse). The models are optional: without them the rules read alone.
- **Web facts are identity evidence.** A fact that fits or contradicts the customer becomes an evidence group with its citation `[S#]`. Until a Golden Case validates it, web evidence cannot close a pair as NO_MATCH or raise it above PROBABLE_MATCH; it supports, conflicts and orders review. The same holds for attached news, where it is built: an age, birth date or gender that rules read next to the Profile person's name is compared with the customer's. Such a value weighs less than the Profile's own field (a full birth date is `MEDIUM`, not `HIGH`), does not count twice when the Profile says the same, and is a conflict only when the Profile's own field does not agree with the customer. Only rule readings are used; a model reading is not evidence.
- **One event, one piece of evidence.** Reports of the same party, event time and charge are one event; the summary lists every source of it. A fact stated by one source is never generalised to all.
- **Every source used has a summary**: publisher, date, title, then the people and events in fixed sentence patterns, each phrase a slot of a cited sentence. Rewording never swaps actor and object, and the event date is never the publication date.
- **Source condition, legal stage and AML relevance** keep their own rules over structured Profile fields; the digest does not set them.
- **The legal stage in attached news is a vote.** Two optional stage models read it beside the rules (`IStageReader`): the person-stage classifier in the process (ONNX), and a large language model on an endpoint inside the bank (Ollama). With both answering, the stage at least two of the three give wins; all three different is UNKNOWN for a reviewer to read. With one model the rules' stage stands and a model that differs only orders review. Without models the rules read alone, as before. Every reader's stage, and the language model's quote, stays on the condition, so the reviewer sees who said what. A model that fails or times out drops out of that Case's vote; it never blocks the analysis.

## Consequences

- The news digest, its codes and its known limits are specified in the [feature spec](../feature/watchlist-and-adverse-information.md#news-digest); web identity evidence will add evidence groups and codes there and in [identity adjudication](../feature/identity-adjudication.md).
- Search queries carry the listed person's name to outside sites; this is public list data, logged per Case.
- Model accuracy is measured on the bank's annotated judgments and press releases, outside the repository, and recorded in the feature spec; there is no Golden Case for web evidence yet.
- With the stage models configured, a revision depends on two models and the language model's answer: temperature 0 and a fixed seed make it repeatable on one server and model build, not across builds. The condition names each reader that voted, not its model version.
- The language model's call takes seconds to minutes inside the synchronous analysis; its timeout (`StageModels:LlmTimeoutSeconds`, default 60 s) stays below the batch deadline (`Batch:Timeout`, 2 minutes), so a slow server drops the language model from the vote instead of timing out the job. Any failure of a model reader drops that reader.
- Whether the language model may be used at all is open under [ADR-0015](0015-licences.md): it stays off unless configured.
