# Copies register (template)

Every copy of a data bundle, encrypted or being unpacked, gets a row; a removal (`bundle.py remove`) is applied to every
row's copy and noted. Keep the filled register with the archives, not in the repository. No password, name or text here.

| Host | Date | Bundle (original / pseudonymised) | Archive file and its sha256 | Manifest sha256 | Where it lives | Removed documents applied (sha256 of the id, date) | Deleted on |
